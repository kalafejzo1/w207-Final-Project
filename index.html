Revenue is extremely right-skewed

Almost half of all listings earn under $10K a year, while the mean ($23K) is pulled up by a long tail. That's why we trimmed the 1st and 99th percentiles before modeling.

Where the best model works, and where it breaks

Neural net test R² by listing price tier

The model is strong for mid- and high-priced listings (R² 0.75–0.83) but fails on the top tier. Luxury listings are rare and volatile, which points to a separate model for that segment as the next step.

How we built it

Define the target

Estimated annual revenue = nightly price × (365 − available nights), from Inside Airbnb LA listings.

Clean & trim

Parsed prices, dropped invalid rows, removed the 1st/99th percentile tails and minimum stays over 365 nights.

Join Zillow

Exact, partial, city-level, then fuzzy name matching linked 76% of listings to a neighborhood home value. The rest were median-imputed.

Model

Scaled numeric + one-hot categorical features. Compared linear regression, random forest, gradient boosting and tuned neural nets on MAE, RMSE and R².
